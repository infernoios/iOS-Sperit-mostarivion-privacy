The loop

Collect. Absorb. Evolve.

One rule runs the whole game: size decides who eats whom. You steer freely across a top-down arena — drag anywhere or switch on the virtual joystick in settings.

01

Collect Essence

Four tiers of Essence drift through the wilds, each worth a different chunk of your evolution bar. Soul Magnet pulls them in once you have earned it.

02

Absorb the smaller

Touch a creature under your size and it becomes yours — plus three points of evolution fill. Touch something bigger and it takes your size instead.

03

Evolve three times

Every level demands at least three evolutions to count as won. Each transformation raises your size ceiling, changes your beast and wakes a new power.

Questions

Before you download

Do I need an account or a sign-up?

No. Mostarivion has no login, no registration and no profile server. The name and avatar you set in the app are stored on your device and never leave it.

Does it work offline?

Completely. All 30 levels, the bestiary, the shop, awards, daily tasks and the leaderboard run on device — the game never asks for a network connection.

Is the leaderboard made of real players?

No, and the app does not pretend otherwise. Because Mostarivion is fully offline, the top-100 board is a fixed local ladder you climb with your own score. Nothing about you is uploaded or compared with other people.

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

Neither. Coins are earned by playing — levels, awards, daily tasks and the 24-hour chest bonus — and everything in the shop is bought with those coins. There is no advertising SDK in the app.

Where is my progress stored, and what happens if I delete the app?

Progress lives in a local database inside the app's own sandbox on your device. Deleting the app deletes that database and all progress with it — there is no cloud copy to restore from.

What does it take to run?

An iPhone on iOS 16.6 or later. Mostarivion is an iPhone-only game — it plays in portrait and adapts to every screen size from the smallest iPhone upward.
